COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT PROJECT · CEP-241

Water Pollution Awareness & River Clean-Up

Our rivers and lakes are choking on plastic. We are a student team working with the local community to clean one waterbody and teach people how to keep it clean.

Take the pledgeSee our plan

The problem

Local freshwater bodies are degrading because of plastic accumulation, illegal dumping and weak oversight.

  • Harm to fish, birds and other aquatic life
  • Health risks for people living nearby, such as waterborne disease and mosquitoes
  • Pollution travels downstream and affects other communities

Our aim: reduce water pollution through public awareness and a hands-on clean-up drive.

What we are doing

1

Awareness drive

Workshops, posters and door-to-door talks in schools and housing areas, so people stop pollution at the source.

2

River clean-up

A safe, well-equipped clean-up of the shoreline with waste sorted into plastic, other dry waste and hazardous items.

3

Impact & reflection

We weigh the waste, count the people reached, and report what we learned.

Who is involved

  • Municipal authority / Gram Panchayat: waste vehicle and disposal permission
  • Local NGO: safety guidance and tools
  • Student volunteers: clean-up and outreach
  • Faculty mentor: guidance and approval

Site: Khadakwasla Lake

3-week timeline

Week 1 · Planning
Site survey, safety check, permission from local authority, NGO tie-up.
Week 2 · Awareness
Door-to-door campaign, poster drive, school talks, volunteer briefing.
Week 3 · Clean-up
Clean-up drive, waste segregation, handover to the municipal body.
After the event · Reporting
Impact assessment, activity book, project report and presentation.

Dates: 5 October 2026 to 31 October 2026

Safety first

Before

Safety briefing for everyone and a site check for slippery banks and sharp debris.

During

First-aid point, gloves, masks and boots compulsory, nobody works alone or enters deep water.

Hazardous waste

Glass, medical waste and chemicals are handled only by trained municipal staff.
